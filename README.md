# Safe Haven 🌳🦆

A tiny, gentle puzzle game made for **GitHub Game Off 2026** (theme: **SHIFT**).

You are the house duck, a little green-and-gold robot marked **589**. Lost friends are scattered around the haven. Find them and guide them home to the little **Tree of Life** with the golden fruit. When everyone is home, the fruit glows.

## How SHIFT works
The haven exists on two layers: the **Day shift** and the **Night shift**. Press **Shift** to flip between them.
- Green hedges are only solid by day; violet star-walls are only solid by night.
- Some friends only show up by day, others only by night.
- Friends who are following you shift layers with you.

## Kind words
Finding and bringing friends home shows a gentle affirmation (a Day set and a Night set). When everyone is home, the Tree of Life shares a classic quote (Lao Tzu, Marcus Aurelius (one line adapted to "a good person"), Seneca, Epictetus, Heraclitus) with its source.

## Controls
- Arrow keys / WASD: move
- **Shift**: flip Day ⇄ Night
- R: restart
- M: mute / unmute sounds
- When everyone is home, the home door opens: step through it to finish.
